# Student guide

Risetra guides a student from an early research concern to a proposal that is ready for
human review. The student remains responsible for every claim, source, and decision.

## Before opening Risetra

Prepare what you already have. An unfinished idea is enough.

- Your study program and institution.
- A topic or situation that concerns you.
- The object, people, place, text, system, or phenomenon you may study.
- Any supervisor notes and campus proposal guidelines.
- Source files or links you already trust.
- Honest limits: time, budget, equipment, participant access, and data access.

Do not prepare participant identities, confidential records, or restricted data unless
your institution permits them to be processed with your Codex account.

## From setup to proposal

1. **Open Risetra.** On macOS, double-click `Start Risetra.command`.
2. **Describe your research situation.** Complete the four short setup steps. You can
   choose Bahasa Indonesia for the dashboard, conversation, and proposal.
3. **Select Save and start.** Risetra opens the Guidance page and starts a Codex session
   in the proposal workspace.
4. **Answer one question at a time.** The guide explains why a question matters, records
   the decision, shows missing evidence, and lists any document it changed.
5. **Build the evidence trail.** Search Crossref for candidate literature or add sources
   manually. Read each source and mark it verified only after checking its metadata,
   access, and relevance.
6. **Review the documents.** Codex drafts the research charter, research questions, and
   Chapters 1–3. Edit anything that does not represent your meaning. Every edit returns
   the document to `DRAFT`.
7. **Check alignment.** Make sure each research question has one matching objective,
   method, and expected evidence output.
8. **Record human review.** Mark a document `REVIEWED` or `APPROVED` with the reviewer
   name and a meaningful note only after it has actually been read.
9. **Run Checks.** Resolve errors first, then inspect warnings about missing or
   unverified evidence.
10. **Export the proposal.** Download Markdown, DOCX, or PDF. Approved-only export is the
    default; reviewed documents may be included explicitly.

## What the Codex guide does

The guide reads the workspace, applies the selected research modules, updates proposal
artifacts, and resumes one research thread across sessions. It explains decisions in the
student's interaction language and stops at a proposal-ready Chapter 3 plus seminar
readiness.

The guide does not invent sources, research gaps, quotations, page numbers, participant
data, findings, or Chapters 4–5. Literature search returns metadata candidates, not proof
that a source supports a claim.

## If something stops

- **Codex unavailable:** run `codex login`, then reload Risetra.
- **A response takes too long:** use Stop, revise the instruction, and continue the same
  session.
- **The session is confused:** use New session. Proposal files remain in the workspace.
- **An export is blocked:** open Checks and complete the required review evidence.
- **A source cannot be accessed:** keep it unverified and do not cite it as evidence.

All proposal files remain readable Markdown and JSON inside the workspace, so no work is
locked inside the interface.
