# Experimental research pack

Define the intervention, comparison, assignment mechanism, outcome, timing, power or
precision rationale, protocol, manipulation or fidelity check, blinding where feasible,
exclusion policy, analysis plan, stopping rule, adverse-event handling, and ethics.

Separate confirmatory outcomes from exploratory analysis. Do not modify exclusions or
outcomes after observing results without documenting the change.

The student must explain which design feature supports a causal interpretation and
which threats remain.
