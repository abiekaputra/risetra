# Survey research pack

Define the target population, frame, sampling or recruitment, administration mode,
constructs, item source, cognitive or pilot testing, response-quality checks,
nonresponse handling, weighting where justified, analysis, consent, and privacy.

Convenience respondents do not automatically represent a population. A large response
count does not repair frame or measurement bias.

The student must explain who could not enter the sample and how that affects each claim.
