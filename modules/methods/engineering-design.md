# Engineering and design research pack

Use this pack when the research contribution includes an artifact, system, process, or
design intervention evaluated against explicit requirements.

Trace stakeholder needs to measurable requirements, design alternatives, constraints,
implementation decisions, verification procedures, validation context, comparison
baseline, failure modes, and reproducibility evidence.

A working prototype alone is not a research result. The proposal must explain which
knowledge claim the artifact helps evaluate and why the chosen measurements support
that claim.

The student must distinguish product acceptance criteria from the research question
and state what evidence would falsify the expected improvement.
