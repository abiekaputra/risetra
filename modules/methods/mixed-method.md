# Mixed-method research pack

Use this pack only when integrating qualitative and quantitative evidence answers the
question better than either component alone.

Name the design sequence, priority, component-specific sampling, collection, analysis,
and the exact integration point. Explain whether one component builds, explains,
expands, or triangulates the other. Create a joint display or another explicit
integration artifact.

Two independent studies in one document are not automatically mixed-method research.
Conflicting findings must be examined rather than averaged away.

The student must explain what becomes knowable through integration that was not
knowable from the components separately.
