# Literature-based research pack

Define the review question, databases, search strings, date and language limits,
inclusion and exclusion criteria, screening process, quality assessment, extraction
fields, synthesis method, and update date.

Do not call an informal collection “systematic.” Deduplication, screening decisions,
and excluded full texts require reproducible records. A search ending with few results
does not by itself establish novelty.

The student must explain how the search could miss relevant evidence and how that risk
limits the conclusion.
