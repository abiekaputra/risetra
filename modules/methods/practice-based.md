# Practice-based research pack

Use this pack when creative practice is part of the inquiry rather than merely its
illustration. Define the research question, practice context, iterative process,
documentation protocol, reflective method, critical references, audience or expert
engagement, and how the artifact contributes evidence.

The finished work alone does not reveal the research process. Preserve iterations,
rejected decisions, situated reflections, and criteria used to interpret the work.

The student must explain what became knowable through making and how another reviewer
can inspect the evidence behind that interpretation.
