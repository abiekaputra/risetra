# Doctrinal legal research pack

Use this pack when the question interprets, systematizes, or evaluates law through
authoritative legal materials. Define jurisdiction, time boundary, hierarchy of
authority, source-selection rules, interpretive approach, treatment of conflicting
authority, and the legal proposition being examined.

Separate what the law states, how an authority interprets it, and the researcher's
normative argument. Frequency of decisions does not establish authority or meaning.

The student must explain why each source is authoritative and how the interpretation
would change if a competing authority were preferred.
