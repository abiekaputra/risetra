# Qualitative research pack

Use this pack when the question seeks meaning, experience, process, interpretation, or
context that cannot be reduced to predefined numeric variables.

Specify the case or phenomenon, participant or material selection, access, consent,
collection procedure, researcher role, analytic approach, coding or interpretation
process, reflexivity, negative-case handling, and an audit trail.

Do not claim statistical representativeness. Explain transferability through context
and sampling logic. Do not call a few open-ended survey fields a qualitative design
without an appropriate collection and analysis procedure.

The student must explain why the selected voices or materials can illuminate the
question and whose perspective remains absent.
