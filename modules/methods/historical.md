# Historical research pack

Define the period, place, actors, archive or corpus, provenance rules, source criticism,
contextualization, corroboration, and treatment of silence or survival bias.

Distinguish primary evidence, later interpretation, and the researcher's inference.
Absence from an archive is not proof that an event or perspective did not exist.

The student must explain who created each source, for which purpose, under which
conditions, and which voices are unlikely to survive in the selected record.
