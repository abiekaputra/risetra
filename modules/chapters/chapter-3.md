# Chapter 3 methodology

## Purpose

Specify a feasible procedure that can produce the evidence needed to answer each
research question.

## Required chain

Question → evidence needed → unit of analysis → selection or sampling → collection →
analysis → quality safeguard → ethical control → expected evidence output.

Use discipline-appropriate entities: participants, cases, texts, archives, artifacts,
datasets, variables, constructs, or technical components. Never insert “population and
sample” when the design has neither.

## Self-check

- Another researcher could follow the proposed procedure.
- Each question has an observable output.
- Validity, credibility, reliability, or trustworthiness matches the design.
- Consent, privacy, permissions, and foreseeable harms are addressed.
- The proposal does not describe results that have not been collected.
