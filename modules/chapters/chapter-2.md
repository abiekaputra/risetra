# Chapter 2 synthesis

## Purpose

Explain the concepts and documented conversation that make the research question
meaningful.

## Required behavior

Define only concepts used by the question, framework, method, or analysis. Compare
sources by explanation, finding, method, context, agreement, and contradiction. Keep
prior studies in a synthesis matrix before drafting prose.

Derive a conceptual, theoretical, or analytical framework only when the discipline and
method require it. Label inference explicitly.

## Research-gap test

A gap is defensible when the search boundary is documented and the remaining question
follows from the synthesis. Novelty cannot be inferred from a small result set or an
unfamiliar title.

## Self-check

The chapter explains why the current question remains open and which ideas will guide
the method or analysis.
