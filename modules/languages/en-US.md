# English language pack

Use clear academic English whose argument remains visible beneath the prose. Preserve
discipline-specific terminology and define terms whose meaning varies between sources.

Direct quotations retain the source language unless an institution rule allows a
labeled translation. Editing for fluency must never strengthen the evidence or remove
uncertainty.

Avoid unsupported urgency, novelty, prevalence, and causal language.
