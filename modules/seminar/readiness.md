# Seminar readiness

The proposal is ready for rehearsal when Chapters 1–3 are internally consistent, each
material claim maps to a verified source, and the method can produce the evidence
needed for every question.

Generate a concise decision map: problem boundary, gap, question, objective, framework,
method, expected evidence, limitation, and ethical control. Create examiner questions
that test those decisions rather than asking the student to recite paragraphs.

The student passes rehearsal when they can explain why each major decision was chosen,
which alternative was rejected, and what the proposal cannot claim.
