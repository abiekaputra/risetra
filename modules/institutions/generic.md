# Generic institution pack

This fallback pack supplies no institution-specific heading, margin, citation, ethics,
or seminar rule. Students must obtain the current official guide from their institution
and record its version before final formatting.

Local rules may rename or reorder sections. They may not remove evidence traceability,
question-to-method alignment, academic-integrity controls, or human review gates.

When a local rule conflicts with a method requirement, surface the conflict for human
resolution instead of silently choosing one.
